Privacy Policy

5K · Effective October 17, 2026

This policy covers the 5K website (5k.co) and jarvis-cron, a private automation app that 5K's owner, Dave Conklin, uses to manage his own email, calendar and files. jarvis-cron is not offered to the public. The only Google account it accesses is the one that authorized it.

What jarvis-cron accesses

  • Gmail: sender, subject, labels and a short preview of messages, so it can apply labels, archive old promotions, move spam and phishing out of the inbox, and create drafts. It never sends email.
  • Google Calendar: event titles, times and organizers, to count meetings and to decline obvious spam invitations without notifying the sender.
  • Google Drive: it writes a daily activity digest and monthly bank statement exports to specific folders the owner chose. It does not read other files.

How the data is used

Data is used only to provide these features to the account owner. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and jarvis-cron does not use it to train AI models.

Sharing

  • AI classification: for a small number of emails the rules can't decide on, the sender, subject and the first 200 characters are sent to xAI's API to classify as spam or not. Nothing more of the message is sent.
  • ClickUp: summary counts (for example "12 tasks done") and payment notices are posted to 5K's private ClickUp workspace. No email contents are posted.
  • Nothing else is shared with third parties, except where required by law.

Storage and retention

jarvis-cron keeps message IDs, run times and short activity logs on 5K's own computer and in a private GitHub repository's job cache. Detailed logs are deleted after 14 days. Credentials are kept in encrypted secret storage and are never published.

Google API Services

jarvis-cron's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. On request, all stored data is deleted.

The 5K website

When you contact us through 5k.co, we use what you send only to reply to you. We may use standard analytics to understand site traffic.
